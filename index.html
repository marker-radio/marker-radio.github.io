Marker Radio Ohio

Ohio history, spoken while you drive. Turn it on, allow location, and it tells the story of the town, marker, or person you are passing.

Can we make this better?

Found a problem, a wrong fact, or a story we missed? Email markerradio.app@gmail.com. That note is how the next version gets better.

Privacy

The app uses your location on the phone so it knows which story to play. Location is not sold, not used for ads, and not tied to an account. There is no sign-in.

To ask a question or request that we delete an email you sent us, write to markerradio.app@gmail.com.
